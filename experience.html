QUESTIONS OF MY OWN

Independent research

The Wait Gap

My A+ thesis combines 3D modeling and discrete-event simulation to explore public restroom layouts. Modeled layouts reduced women’s waits by 54 seconds with a six-second increase for men. The simulator has had beta users in three countries.

Read the restroom case study →

Can My Menstrual Cycle Predict the Stock Cycle?

I classified 15,000+ health observations with an F1 score above 0.80. Dynamic Time Warping found no evidence of menstrual–market alignment—a negative finding worth reporting.

Read the project ↗

Pink Tax: India vs. Japan

I scraped 100,000 product observations across three marketplaces. The mean premium was +12.9%, the median was zero, and women paid more in fewer than 40% of matched pairs. The average alone didn’t tell the whole story.
